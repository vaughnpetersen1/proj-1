# Client Intake — what to collect the moment a deal closes

Copy this checklist into your text thread with the client. The faster you fill it,
the faster I ship the site — everything here in hand = site built same day.

## The must-haves

- [ ] **Deposit received** ($150 — nothing starts without it)
- [ ] Business name (exactly as they want it spelled)
- [ ] What they do, in their words (2–3 sentences is plenty)
- [ ] Services + prices (or "call for quote" if they don't post prices)
- [ ] Phone number + whether they prefer calls or texts
- [ ] Address (or service area if mobile)
- [ ] Hours
- [ ] **The one action**: what should a visitor DO? (call / text / book / walk in)

## The nice-to-haves (site's better with them, fine without)

- [ ] 5–15 photos of their work (phone pics are fine — I'll make them look good)
- [ ] Logo (any format; no logo = I'll do a clean text treatment)
- [ ] Instagram/Facebook links
- [ ] Colors they love or hate ("no pink" saves a revision round)
- [ ] Their Google Maps listing link (for the map embed + review count)
- [ ] Anything they want to brag about (years in business, awards, "as seen in")

## Then paste it all to me

Start a session and drop everything in — messy is fine, screenshots of the text
thread are fine. Tell me:

1. The niche vibe they want (closest existing demo is a good reference)
2. Deadline you promised (default: 48 hours)

I'll build it under `service/clients/<business-name>/` and give you a preview to
send them. Two revision rounds included — collect their feedback in one batch
each round instead of drip-feeding, and we'll never blow the deadline.

## At delivery

- [ ] Final $150 collected
- [ ] Pitch the care plan: *"$10/month and you never think about this site again —
      hosting, edits, updates, all handled. Want me to set that up?"*
- [ ] Ask for a referral: *"Know anyone else who needs one? I'll knock $50 off
      their price and give you $25 when they go live."*
- [ ] Log both payments in `tracker/ledger.csv`
