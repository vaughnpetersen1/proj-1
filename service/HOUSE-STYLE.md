# House Style — rules for anything a client or lead will see

Set by the boss. Not suggestions.

1. **No emojis on client storefronts.** Ever. Icons are inline SVG or nothing.
   Emojis render differently on every device and read as DIY.
2. **No client-facing page goes out without real photos.** Build the skeleton
   anytime, but nothing gets SENT to a lead or client with stock photos posing
   as their work. Stand-in photos must be labeled as stand-ins on the page,
   and the goal is always: real photos in before it's in front of them.
3. **Outreach voice** (see outreach/batch1_messages.md): reads like a text
   from a real person. Short sentences. No brochure language.
4. **Never publish unconfirmed facts about a client's business as fact** —
   placeholder prices/hours are marked as placeholders on the page.
