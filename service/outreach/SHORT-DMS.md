# SHORT DMs — the rewrite (2026-07-24)

**Why:** 3 separate people told the boss our messages read as automated.
They were ~70 words with a price, a link and a pitch. That's a flyer.

**The rule now: message 1 is ONE question. Under 25 words. No link. No price.
No pitch.** People ignore pitches. People answer questions.

Tells that scream "bot" (avoid all of these in msg 1):
- em dashes, perfect punctuation, two+ sentences of value prop
- a link before they've said anything
- "I noticed you don't have a website, which means when people google..."
- listing what's included ($300, 48 hours, tap-to-call, hours, photos)

Tells that read human:
- lowercase start, contractions, a little imperfect
- one thought, then stop
- asking something you'd actually text a stranger
