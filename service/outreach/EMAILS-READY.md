## Deliverability notes (real, matters)

- Send these **one at a time, spaced out** — not five in one minute. Gmail
  flags bursts from a fresh sender.
- This inbox is mostly unread Facebook notifications, which means it has
  almost no sending history. That makes it a "cold" sender and more likely to
  land in spam.
- If these go nowhere, **the phone is better than email for this market.**
  Contractors and shop owners answer calls; they don't read cold email.
