# Finding leads on your phone — 5 minutes, unlimited supply

## Why you do this instead of Claude

Claude's web search finds businesses that RANK on Google. Ranking basically
requires having a website. So the businesses we want — the ones with no site —
are the exact ones Claude's search can't see. Worse, Claude is blocked from
Facebook/Instagram feeds, so it can't confirm anyone is actually active.

You have both. Google Maps tells you instantly whether a website exists, and
you can see the date of their last FB/IG post. What takes Claude an hour of
searching takes you 5 minutes of scrolling.

## The method

1. Open **Google Maps** on your phone.
2. Search a niche + town: `landscaping Alexandria MN`, `tree service Duluth`,
   `pressure washing Fargo`. Any niche, any town.
3. Tap a business. Look at the buttons row.
   - **No "Website" button = LEAD.** That's the whole test.
   - If there IS a website button, tap it. Loads slow, looks like 2009, breaks
     on your phone? Still a lead — that's the REDESIGN pitch.
4. Qualify it in 20 seconds:
   - Reviews in the last year? (proof they're alive)
   - Tap through to their Facebook. **Top post from this year?** If the last
     post is 2023, skip — dead business, house rule 9.
5. Screenshot it or note: name, phone, niche, town.
6. Send the short opener from SHORT-DMS.md:
   > hey is this [name]? quick question, do you guys have a website?
   > couldn't find one

## What makes a GOOD lead (in order)

1. **No website** + posted this year + owner answers their own phone
2. **Bad website** (2009-looking, broken on mobile) + active
3. Solo operator / small crew — they decide, no gatekeeper
4. Service business where photos matter: landscaping, tree, detailing,
   barber, salon, pressure washing, remodeling, junk hauling

## What to SKIP

- Anything with a real, modern site (don't pitch "you have no website")
- Franchises and 50-employee companies (gatekeepers, agencies, slow)
- No posts in over a year — dead, house rule 9
- Businesses only reachable through LawnStarter/Thumbtack/GreenPal —
  they hide the phone number, you can't reach the owner

## Then hand it to Claude

Paste the list — name, niche, town, phone. Claude will:
- double-check for a website you might have missed
- build them a real mockup so your message becomes
  "I already made you one" instead of "want one?"

## Realistic pace

10 minutes of scrolling = 5-8 qualified leads. That's a week of outreach
at 5 sends a day. You'll never run dry again, and you'll never wait on
Claude for lead supply.
