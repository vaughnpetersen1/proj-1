# Learning to use Claude at full potential — reading list + our own gaps

Researched 2026-07-29 at boss's request.

---

## Part 1 — The people actually worth watching

Ranked by usefulness **to us specifically** (a two-person shop selling
websites), not by follower count.

### 1. Nick Saraev — start here
Described as the most technically rigorous Claude Code educator on YouTube.
His **"CLAUDE CODE FULL COURSE 4 HOURS: Build & Sell (2026)"** has ~1.4M views
and covers agent teams, git worktrees, cloud deployment and MCP servers.
**Why him first:** the whole framing is *build AND sell*, which is literally
our business. Most creators only teach building.

### 2. IndyDevDan — for how to THINK about it
Opinionated, principle-first walkthroughs on agentic engineering. Best once
you want sub-agents and real agent stacks rather than one-off prompts.
**Why:** teaches the mental model, not just button-pushing. This is the
channel that changes how you delegate to me.

### 3. Matthew Berman — for staying current
Fast, comparison-heavy reviews of new Claude features as they ship.
**Why:** low effort, keeps us from missing capabilities we already pay for.

### 4. Cole Medin — full agent stacks
Good next step after IndyDevDan when we want multi-agent setups.

### 5. Anthropic Academy + official docs
The canonical reference, vendor-led. Boring but correct — good for settling
"can Claude actually do X" arguments.

### Business-angle videos (closest to our situation)
- "How I Would Build A One Person Claude AI Business In 2026"
- "FULL 3 HOUR COURSE: Automate Your Business With Claude AI (2026)"
- "How I'd Build a One-Person AI Business Using Only Claude ($0 - $1M)"
- "Claude Just Changed Web Design Forever (Tutorial)"

### Also mentioned, lower priority for us
Nate Herk, Simon Scrapes, Jack Roberts, Chase Hannegan, Edmund Yong,
AY Automate's "30 Days of Claude Code Challenge" (structured daily ramp).

**Honest caveat:** the AI-tutorial space is full of people who make money
teaching AI rather than using it. Nick Saraev and IndyDevDan came up
repeatedly as substance. Watch one, apply one thing, then go back to work —
don't binge ten hours instead of sending DMs.

## Part 2 — Where WE are underusing Claude (no video needed)

Honest self-assessment of what's been left on the table in this project:

### 1. Speed on replies is our #1 unused weapon
The moment any lead replies, paste it to me raw. I can have their real
personalized site built and screenshotted in minutes. We've done this for
512, MG, MN Detail, Kz's — every time it made the pitch dramatically
stronger. **The gap is latency, not capability.** A reply that sits 12 hours
is a lost deal.

### 2. Paste messy things — don't clean them up first
Screenshots, half-typed notes, a Facebook page dump, "this guy said X idk."
I handle raw input fine. Time spent formatting for me is wasted time.

### 3. Tell me when something looks bad
The MG site went from mediocre to genuinely good the moment the boss said
"these look low quality." I can't see design taste unless told. **Criticism
is high-value input, not rudeness.**

### 4. I can run the full loop, not just pieces
research -> build -> screenshot -> verify it renders -> commit -> push.
Ask for the outcome ("build X and show me"), not the individual steps.

### 5. I self-verify when asked
"Check your own work" / "show me a screenshot" catches real bugs. That's how
we caught the invisible About/Reviews sections on the MG page before it ever
reached a client.

### 6. Standing preferences belong in CLAUDE.md
Anything said twice should become a house rule so it survives new sessions.
Already done: no emojis, short DMs, activity-verified leads. Add more freely.

### 7. What I genuinely CANNOT do (stop waiting on these)
- See inside Facebook/Instagram feeds (proxy blocks them) -> boss checks
  post dates, 3 seconds
- Run while boss sleeps — I only execute on a message. No background work.
- Access boss's computer, or send DMs/emails automatically
- Hold or spend money
