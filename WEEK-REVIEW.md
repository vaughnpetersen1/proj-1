## CLAUDE'S SELF-GRADE

**Build quality: 8/10.** The premium standard is genuinely good and the
generator makes it repeatable. Lost points because the first versions were
template-y and it took the boss telling me they looked low quality — I should
have held that bar from the start.

**Lead quality: 5/10.** This was the week's real failure. I handed over leads
that turned out to have websites (4 of 6 in batch 4) and some I couldn't prove
were active. Root cause: I inferred activity from directory timestamps instead
of dated evidence, and my web search structurally favors businesses that *have*
websites. Fixed mid-week, but I shipped sloppy work first.

**Judgment: 8/10.** Held the line on the $2k, on not scraping Facebook, and on
not auto-sending email — all three protect us. Correctly diagnosed that the
70-word DM was the conversion blocker.

**Overall: 7/10.** Strong systems, real quality jump, one bad batch of leads.
