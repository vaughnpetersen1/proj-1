# Flip Guide — power tools, automotive, tech (boss's lanes)

Compiled 2026-07-10 from reseller-community research + flip consensus.
Your lanes are genuinely the three best flip categories that exist — fast
turnover, deep supply, and you can judge condition. This is the playbook.

## Turnover ranking (fastest cash first)

| Rank | Category | Typical days to sell | Why |
|------|----------|---------------------|-----|
| 1 | Apple gear (AirPods Pro, Apple Watch, iPads) | 1–3 days | Endless demand, ships in an envelope |
| 2 | Milwaukee / DeWalt cordless tools + batteries | 1–5 days | Tradespeople are locked into battery platforms and always buying |
| 3 | Game consoles & controllers (Switch, PS5 pads) | 2–7 days | Constant demand, easy to test |
| 4 | Business laptops (ThinkPad T-series), Bose/Sony headphones | ~1 week | Steady buyers, good margins |
| 5 | Auto parts with fitment (OEM lights, OBD2 scanners, car audio) | 1–3 weeks | Slower but fattest margins; less competition |

## Power tools (your lane #1) — the research says this is the sweet spot

- **Impact drivers are king.** Milwaukee M18, DeWalt 20V, Makita LXT sell
  within days at 40–80% margins. Milwaukee ("red tools") moves fastest —
  list one and expect messages within hours.
- **Combo kits with batteries + charger** outsell bare tools and command
  premiums. Batteries are half the value — a genuine M18 5.0Ah battery
  flips by itself, cheap to ship.
- **Circular saws and DeWalt 12" sliding miter saws** are perennial sellers.
- **Sourcing:** FB Marketplace lowballs (stale listings = leverage, you know
  this move now), garage/estate sales, pawn shops that overstocked.
- **The one big risk: stolen tools.** Walk away from filed-off serials,
  brand-new-in-box multiples at too-good prices, or a seller with ten
  drills and no chargers. Stolen goods can get your accounts banned and
  worse. Buy from people whose story makes sense.

## Automotive (lane #2)

- Fast + shippable: **OBD2 scanners** (Autel/BlueDriver), **car audio**
  (head units, amps), **jump starter packs**, torque wrenches, OEM key fobs.
- Fat margins: **OEM headlights/taillights** — put year/make/model in the
  title ("fitment") and they sell to people who NEED them.
- Local-only (too heavy to ship): wheel/tire sets, tonneau covers, hitches.
  Fine flips, but they tie up space like the Jäger fridge would have.
- **OEM always outsells aftermarket.** Same part, double the price.
- Avoid: anything involving catalytic converters (stolen-goods minefield),
  airbags (liability), untested electronics modules.

## Technology (lane #3)

- **AirPods Pro are the fastest flip in existence** — but the #1 faked item
  on earth. Learn the tells (serial check on Apple's site, build quality,
  pairing animation). Buy in person, verify before paying.
- **Phones/iPads: check activation lock BEFORE money changes hands.**
  Settings must be signed out of iCloud/Google. IMEI check for blacklist.
  A locked device is a paperweight, no exceptions.
- Reliable movers: Switch/Switch OLED, PS5 controllers, Bose QC / Sony
  XM4-XM5 headphones, mechanical keyboards, ThinkPad T480/X-series.
- **Seasonal cheat code: TI-84 calculators.** Buy all summer at $30-50,
  sell $80-110 in August/September back-to-school. Every year. Like clockwork.
- MN seasonal bonus: snowblowers and generators bought in summer sell for
  2x in October. Big/local but you have the truck-guy advantage.
