# Decision Log

Big calls get written down here with the reasoning we had AT THE TIME, so we
can grade ourselves honestly later instead of rewriting history.

---

## 2026-07-24 — Hold the $2,000. Do not deploy capital yet.

**Situation:** Boss has $2k available and offered to put it toward the goal.
Day 11 of the push. Revenue $0. Spend to date $182 (see tracker/ledger.csv).

**Decision:** Keep the $2k uninvested. Claude does not take control of funds.
Work the existing lead list with the new short DMs first.

**Reasoning:**
1. The goal is $200/MONTH. One $300 site clears it. Spending $2,000 to earn
   $200 is a bad trade when the same $200 is reachable for free.
2. Capital buys scale, not product-market fit. We have not yet proven the
   offer converts — zero clients closed.
3. We diagnosed the real blocker TODAY: three separate leads said our DMs
   read as automated. We rewrote them (SHORT-DMS.md) and have not tested the
   new version even once. That test is free and is the highest-value
   experiment available.
4. Asymmetry: hold and worst case = $0 revenue + $2k in hand. Spend and worst
   case = $0 revenue + no $2k + worse morale.

**Trigger to revisit — deploy only when BOTH are true:**
- At least one client has actually paid, AND
- The new short-DM sequence has produced measurable replies.

**Then spend ~$300-500 on:** a dedicated cold-email sending domain (NOT the
personal Gmail — ban risk), inbox warmup, and Instantly or Smartlead. That is
the real scale lever: hundreds of safe personalized sends/day instead of
hand-texting five. Flipping inventory is the second candidate, slower per
dollar.

**How to grade this later:** If we close a client in the next 2 weeks with $0
extra spend, this was right. If we stall entirely because we lacked reach
(not because the pitch failed), this was too conservative.
